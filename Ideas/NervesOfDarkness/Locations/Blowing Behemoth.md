A huge gas giant with no real surface. The upward winds of the planet prevent the player from falling to the core, and some clouds can be frozen and walkable for the first 20 minutes in the loop. Otherwise, the radiation from the black hole would heat them down due to expansion. It orbits both the star and the black hole.

**Time Loop Mechanics:**
- **Frozen Clouds:** Clouds in the upper layers are "frozen" the first 10 minutes of the loop and dense enough for the player to walk on top of them. 10 minutes in, the upper layer clouds become completely gaseous and can be phased through again thanks to the radiation of Divine Devourer. Frozen Clouds can also be melted by excess use of thrust or presence of fire, and can be broken due to speed.
- **Winds:** The sideways winds of the upper atmosphere will gradually get stronger as Divine Devourer gets bigger. Some buildings may have turbines which will not become available until the turbine is spun at a different amount. Some platforms may even collide with one another if the wind is too strong. Could potentially allow for a platforming puzzle...
- **Daunting Zeppelin:** Rising from the inner layer, a mysterious Zeppelin rises to the upper atmosphere later in the loop. Its doors will not open until a certain wind speed is achieved.

**Main Gimmicks:**
- **[[Reflective Cloud]]:** Winds from the lower atmosphere rise up to reflect the player and the ship from passing it, unless the player enters at 1 m/s.
- **Crystal Platforms:** Where most of the buildings are situated. They float directly on the Reflective Cloud like it's a liquid. Unlike Giant's Deep, the platforms are not sent upward and stay on the Reflective Cloud.

**Places to Explore:**
- **[[Frozen Cloud Campsite]]:** A set of recording tapes and a campfire atop a frozen cloud. The campfire is already on by default, so it's only available the first few minutes of the loop. Don't bother sleeping here, it's a puzzle.
- **[[Frozen Disc Cloud]]:** An audio player that's frozen solid. It becomes lost when the cloud is melted, so the player will have to use their thruster to melt it.
- **Crystal Platform Campsites:** A handful of campfires are scattered throughout the Upper Atmosphere atop Crystal Platforms for the player to sleep if needed.
- **[[Blowing Behemoth Anomaly Lab]]:** Like every other planet, Blowing Behemoth has an Anomaly Lab that shows the player that entering the black hole is possible. Very easily accessible. It is located in the Upper Atmosphere atop a Crystal Platform.
- **Weather Station:** A structure covered in turbines atop a Crystal Platform. The door doesn't open until later in the loop. Hints of the Zeppelin's existence. (NOTE: MOVE TO FRIGID FOG)
- **[[Daunting Zeppelin]]:** A Zeppelin that appears late in the loop and suddenly rises from the lower to the upper layer. Much like the Weather Station, its doors only open if the wind is strong enough, meaning later in the loop. Inside, it tells you the secret to passing through the Reflective Cloud. (NOTE: Die Valkyrie Easter Egg in Zeppelin would be AWESOME)
- **[[Conductive Energy Plant]]:** Research Lab in the core of Blowing Behemoth that unveils the secret to passing through the Reflective Cloud will work for passing the Event Horizon.
