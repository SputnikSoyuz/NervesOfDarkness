The terrestrial planet in orbit around Divine Devourer that's decorated in scarlet flora. This is where the player spawns by default. After the player goes through one loop, Gabbro sets up camp there to check on you.

**Time Loop Mechanics:**
- **The Rising Flood:** As Divine Devourer gets bigger, the tides begin to rise the water levels until it floods most of the planet.

**Main Gimmicks:**
- **Sound-Producing Plants:** There are plants across the planet that play sounds and are used for sound-related puzzles. In some puzzles, the player can manipulate the sound that they play by shining a light on them or using the scout.

**Places to Explore:**
- **Spawn Camp:** The camp in which the player spawns. After the first loop, Gabbro sets up camp and can be talked to. Gabbro may also be able to help hint the player into the right direction when stumped.
- **[[Maroon Meadows Anomaly Lab]]:** Like every other planet, Maroon Meadows has an Anomaly Lab that shows the player that entering the black hole is possible.
- **[[Botany Lab]]:** As its name suggests, it's a lab for studying botany. It informs the player of the Pumpworks, acts as a tutorial grounds for the jackhammer item, and guides the player into reaching the inside of the Frigid Fog Hurricane.
- **[[Pumpworks]]:** A massive pump built to siphon the water from the entire planet. The player will need to use it to access the Abyssal Archives.
-  **[[Cartography Studio]]:** A mapmaking studio that reveals the location of a handful of recorders across the system.
- **[[Abyssal Archives]]:** An underwater library sealed by a door that doesn't open in water. It teaches the player how to solve linguistic puzzles and gives certain sounds played on recorders meanings.
