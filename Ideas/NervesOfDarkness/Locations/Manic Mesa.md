The only planet that orbits Doomed Daylight, the unfortunate star with matter slowly siphoning into the gaping maw of Divine Devourer. Manic Mesa is a hollow planet with cave systems and the ability to withstand high temperature. While Doomed Daylight is doomed to fall into the Event Horizon, Manic Mesa is doomed to fall into its star. This gives the player an opportunity however to safely plunge into the ocean of plasma and survive the core of Doomed Daylight.

**Time Loop Mechanics:**
- **Plunging Into the Star:** Manic Mesa's days are numbered as it struggles to stay in a stable orbit around its star, until eventually it falls into Doomed Daylight, which falls into Divine Devourer. However, this will be a great advantage to solving its central puzzle, which will have players descend with the planet into the core of a star.
