Divine Devourer is a supermassive black hole that along with Doomed Daylight, orbits around the centre of the star system. Three planets revolve around it and its stellar twin: Purple Partition, Maroon Meadows, and Blowing Behemoth. Past the Event Horizon lies The Singularity, where a mysterious new species resides. To reach the Singularity, the player must safely pass the [[Event Horizon]].
