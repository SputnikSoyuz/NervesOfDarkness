The nearest planet in orbit around Divine Devourer, covered in purple rocks and littered with cave systems with beautiful crystals. However, this world is slowly tearing itself apart from the growing black hole's gravitational influence. It's only a matter of time until this world meets its violent fate.

**Time Loop Mechanics:**
- **The Big Split:** Purple Partition has its name for a reason. When Divine Devourer grows, its gravitational influence will eventually be enough to split the planet in half. 

**Main Gimmicks:**
- **Lava:** Geysers and pools of lava can be found throughout the deeper caverns of Purple Partition and will be used as obstacles.

**Places to Explore:**
- **[[Echo Caverns]]:** A cave with quite a loud atmosphere that echoes every sound that's made in it. You have to figure out how to get the echoing to stop.
- **[[Enclosed Caverns]]:** Inside the main cave system are a series of caves closed off by crystals blocking the way. The player will need to use the jackhammer to make noise to shatter them.
- **[[Excavation Outpost]]**: A mining outpost with an excavation device that slowly digs to the lava. If the player comes at the right time, they can go down the bore hole and grab a luminous crystal to see their way through the Glacial Caverns on Frigid Fog.
- **[[Lava Pass]]:** Players can only pass it by jumping over the lava when the black hole is pulling you just enough to not fall in. Then, the player must wait for the planet to break to reach the recorder on the other side.
- **[[Plasma Blast Furnace]]:** At the core of the planet lies a huge blast furnace that's pumping heat to produce resources. If the player turns it off, they can access the Torrid Caverns without dying.
- **[[Purple Partition Anomaly Lab]]:** Like every other planet, Purple Partition has an Anomaly Lab that shows the player that entering the black hole is possible.
- **[[Refraction Caverns]]:** A series of caves with crystals refracting a beam of light onto a point, preventing the player from progressing to the Warped Observatory unless they block the light with a falling stalagmite.
- **[[Torrid Caverns]]:** A cave in which the player cannot enter without dying, unless the Plasma Blast Furnace is disabled. This is the path to the Warped Observatory.
- **[[Warped Observatory]]:** The central puzzle for Purple Partition. An observatory powered by a black hole that can only be accessed
