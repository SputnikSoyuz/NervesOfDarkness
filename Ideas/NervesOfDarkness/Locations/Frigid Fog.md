The Moon of Blowing Behemoth, which is a snowball, Earth-like moon. The player cannot see past the intense blizzards and fog, requiring them to rely on sound and a Signalscope to navigate.

**Time Loop Mechanics:**
- **Winds:** The whipping winds of Frigid Fog grow louder and stronger as the loop goes on, making it harder to hear your surroundings.
- **Tornadoes:** Tornadoes start to form as the loop gradually progresses, blocking some areas of the planet during certain points of time.

**Main Gimmicks:**
- **Thick Fog:** The thick fog makes it impossible to see what's in front of you, forcing the player to feel around the planet with a Signalscope and their ability to hear.

**Places to Explore:**
- [[Abandoned Camp]]: An abandoned campfire that can't be lit up. It's not a puzzle or anything but it has a recorder next to it.
- **[[Tuning Studio]]:** A lab that reveals tuning combinations for later puzzles in the game.
- **[[Frigid Fog Anomaly Lab]]:** Like every other planet, Frigid Fog has an Anomaly Lab that shows the player that entering the black hole is possible.
- **[[Weather Station]]:** A structure covered in turbines can be found on Frigid Fog. The door doesn't open until later in the loop when the wind is strong enough to push the turbines.
- [[Glacial Caverns]]: Dark caverns beneath the blizzard-infested surface where only the luminous crystal can light the way.
