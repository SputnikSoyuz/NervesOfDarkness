**The Universe Before** was the universe prior to the **Outer Wilds Universe** in the [[Eye of the Universe]]'s death/rebirth cycle. This universe was exactly like **Our Universe** in every way except for the following things:
- The Cold War and the Space Race have never stopped, accelerating space exploration. The USSR still exists and continues to compete with the USA.
- Planet Nine exists, but is confirmed to be the Eye of the Universe. It sends out a signal to humanity.
- The people who run Mobius Digital in our timeline are the ones who entered The Eye, as they are NASA Astronauts in this timeline.
- The Universe was born and has died exactly like the Outer Wilds Universe.
- Other divergence points and anomalies that make this timeline plausible that I don't feel like doing any more research on.

**"Humans Created the Eye"** has some in-game evidence, though not enough to prove it definitively; however, there is nothing in the narrative that seems to disprove it.
- The **Outer Wilds Universe**, while not identical, mirrors humanity’s discoveries and knowledge. The real-life explanation for this is that it is a game created by humans, but within the game's lore, it could hold true if humans were to enter the Eye.
- The **Nomai**, **Strangers**, and **Hearthians** represent three aspects of humanity: those who embrace tradition and are driven by fear; those who will sacrifice everything in the pursuit of progress and science; and those who enjoy the simple pleasures of life without concern for the broader world. These three traits are found within humanity, with one often being more dominant than the others.
- There exists a probe containing human images and videos in the OW system known as the **Fig Backer Probe**. Lore-wise, it can be interpreted as a probe humanity launched into the Eye.
- This concept would tie together the various realities (mods) added to the game without creating conflicts. The mod itself would clarify that it does not conflict with Outer Wilds or other mods.
- There are rumours of a hidden planet in both the OW system (the Eye) and our solar system (Planet Nine).

However, it is not the universe we currently inhabit, and here’s why:
- This alternate human universe meets its demise in the same way as the OW universe.
- Planet Nine is confirmed to be the **Eye of the Universe**.
- Historical context allows for a human to enter the Eye.
