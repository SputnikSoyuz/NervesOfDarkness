There is a new alien species that has left tapes across various planets and mysterious buildings. These tapes are **100% audible**. The species communicates and expresses itself entirely through sound. They have five eyes, two fingers, and appear completely black and shadow-like, with glowing eyes.

They possess the ability to warp objects onto the planets, such as tapes designed to lure players into their corridors, as well as the capability to see multiple universes at once. One of their rooms contains numerous monitors displaying alternate universes, including our real-life timeline, other mods, and remnants from older universes such as [[The Universe Before]].

These alien entities reside inside the black hole at [[The Singularity]], where time passes in strange ways and the laws of the universe differ. You will encounter them in their structures once you figure out how to enter.  They absolutely hate the light and are typically passive until a light is shone on them. Players will need to discover this themselves, as their corridors are so dark that a flashlight is necessary to see. The main objective is to figure out how to avoid being killed, which involves stopping the use of your flashlight. Once you utilize your ability to rely on sound to navigate blind areas, you can befriend them.


**Miscellaneous Characteristics:**
- This species also expresses its love through music, typically in the form of symphonies and orchestras.
- Their society functions in a **hivemind collectivist** format, as they are naturally connected to one another, much like how black holes are interconnected. Hence, the title: **Nerves of Darkness**.
- The aliens have **five eyes** that follow a specific pattern: one eye set out a call, two eyes shut it down, three eyes sought to find it, four eyes got to see it, **and five eyes saw it all**.
- Inside their black hole, they can witness multiple timelines since all black holes are interconnected and exist outside the plane of spacetime.
- They communicate through sound, navigate through sound, and despise light. Unlike most organisms, these beings emit sound from their palms, as they do not possess mouths.
- Their method of attack, if you shine a flashlight on them, is to strangle from behind. They can teleport behind you instantly and kill you before you even see them.
- Because you encounter them in complete darkness, these entities don’t have many animations, so an armature of them will suffice.
