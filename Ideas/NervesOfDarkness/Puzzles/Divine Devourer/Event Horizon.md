Rumour Title: Event Horizon
Same for Revealed Title

| Rumour Reveal Location                                                        | Rumour Description                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Default: System Reveal                                                        | Hornfels once told me that the event horizon of a supermassive black hole like this one behaves differently than the primordial black holes seen throughout our home system due to their significantly higher mass. |
| Death: Failure to Cross the Event Horizon                                     | Attempting to enter the event horizon instantly kills me.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Any Black Hole Laboratory (One on Each Planet)                                | A laboratory display device suggests a mysterious structure lies on the other side of the event horizon.                                                                                                            |
| After Discovering the Rule of Tidal Growth in Manic Mesa's Core               | A display device in the [[Particle Accelerator]] shows that the event horizon is safe to cross when Divine Devourer consumes the entire system.                                                                     |
| After Discovering the Rule of Low Velocity in Blowing Behemoth's Core         | A display device in the [[Conductive Energy Plant]] shows that the event horizon is safe to cross if you approach it at the same speed as entering the lower atmosphere in Blowing Behemoth.                        |
| After Discovering the Rule of Planetary Orientation in the Warped Observatory | A display device in the [[Warped Observatory]] shows that the event horizon is safe to cross at roughly the same angle as the inclination of the planets' orbits.                                                   |
| Passing the Event Horizon                                                     | I used the three rules to safely pass the Event Horizon!                                                                                                                                                            |
Passing the Event Horizon is the mod's "Sixth Location" and "Ash Twin Project" equivalent. It is the main curiosity of the system and the central puzzle the player needs to solve. It is also the only puzzle that takes place *at* Divine Devourer (not inside it). Completing the puzzle will give you access to [[The Singularity]], the final area of the mod and the home of the New Species.
