The Pumpworks is a structure on Maroon Meadows designed to siphon the water out of the planet. To get there, the player has to wait for the flood to reach its absolute high and the entrance will open by the turn of a turbine. The Pumpworks can be used to gain access to the [[Abyssal Archives]].
