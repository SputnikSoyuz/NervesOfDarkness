The Abyssal Archives is the central puzzle of Maroon Meadows beneath the seabed of Maroon Meadows. To get there, the player has to use the Pumpworks to drain the water of the entire planet. The Archives teach the player how to understand linguistics puzzles and associate different sounds in recordings with actual meanings.
