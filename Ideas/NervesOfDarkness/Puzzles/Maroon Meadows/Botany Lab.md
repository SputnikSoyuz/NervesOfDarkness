Rumour Title: Overgrown Structure
Revealed Title: Botany Lab

| Rumour Reveal Location    | Rumour Description                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Botany Lab                | I found an overgrown structure on Maroon Meadows.                                                                                               |
| Botany Lab (Scout)        | I used my scout to give the plants inside some light, emitting a wavelength that opens the door.                                                |
| Botany Lab Display Device | A display device in the Botany Lab shows that a pump structure can remove the water and allow the door to function.                             |
| Botany Lab Recording      | A recording in the Botany Lab has a sequence of sounds that seem to point out a path past the Hurricane. I also heard digging in the recording. |

As its name suggests, it's a lab for studying botany. To enter, the plants have to be lit up by the scout to start photosynthesis and grow large enough to emit a certain wavelength that opens the door. It informs the player of the Pumpworks, acts as a tutorial grounds for the jackhammer item, and guides the player into reaching the inside of the Frigid Fog Hurricane.
