The Cartography Studio is a collection of display devices that show marked maps unveiling the location of otherwise hard-to-find recording devices across the system. The building is at a very low elevation and thus is the first to face the rising flood (early in the loop), which then all the display devices and other information inside are inaccessible. All of the recorders that are shown the location of by the Cartography Studio are linguistic puzzles that eventually lead you to the [[Particle Accelerator]].
