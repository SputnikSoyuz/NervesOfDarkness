The Frozen Disc Cloud is a small puzzle only available early in the loop in the high reaches of Blowing Behemoth. Inside the cloud is a frozen media disc, which the player can insert into a disc player. To solve the puzzle, the player has to wait on the frozen cloud until the exact moment for it to thaw. Come too early, it's frozen solid to the cloud. Come too late, the item will never be seen for the rest of the loop. Its recorder is one of five clues on how to reach the Particle Accelerator.
