The Frozen Cloud Campsite is a small puzzle only available early in the loop in the high reaches of Blowing Behemoth. The campfire is not for sleeping, it's a puzzle. Because of the campfire, this cloud melts faster than the others. Because of this, the player must put out the fire before it's too late when arriving, before the recorder becomes lost for the rest of the loop. The recordings eventually lead you to the Particle Accelerator.
