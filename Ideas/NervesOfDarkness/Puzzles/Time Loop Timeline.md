**Frozen Clouds:** Clouds in the upper layers are "frozen" the first 10 minutes of the loop and dense enough for the player to walk on top of them. 10 minutes in, the upper layer clouds become completely gaseous and can be phased through again thanks to the radiation of Divine Devourer. Frozen Clouds can also be melted by excess use of thrust or presence of fire, and can be broken due to speed.
