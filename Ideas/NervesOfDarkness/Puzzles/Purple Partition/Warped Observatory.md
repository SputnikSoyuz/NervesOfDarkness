The Warped Observatory is the central puzzle of Purple Partition. To get there, the player has to use the Plasma Blast Furnace to safely access the Torrid Caverns, which lead to the inside of the Observatory. The purpose of the Observatory is to teach the player that the 45 degree inclination angle is the safest angle to enter the black hole with.
