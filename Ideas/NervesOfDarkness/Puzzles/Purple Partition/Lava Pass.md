The Lava Path is a cave situated near the North Pole on the near of Purple Partition. Players can only pass the lava when Divine Devourer gets close enough to the planet to influence the player's gravity, allowing them to jump higher and farther with less effort across the lava pools. On the other side is another clue that'll eventually lead you to the Particle Accelerator.
