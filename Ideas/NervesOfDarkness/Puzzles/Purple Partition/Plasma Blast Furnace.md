The Plasma Blast Furnace is located within the molten core of Purple Partition. To get there, the player has to get past the [[Refraction Caverns]]. Inside the Plasma Blast Furnace, the player learns how to enter and control the [[Pumpworks]]. Turning off the Blast Furnace makes some areas colder, allowing you to potentially bypass the Torrid Caverns.
