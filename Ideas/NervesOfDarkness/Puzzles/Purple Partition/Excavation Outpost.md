The Excavation Outpost is a mining outpost found in the big cavern on the far side of Purple Partition. It has a excavation drill digging a borehole throughout the loop, until it eventually strikes lava and causes a flood in the entire cavern. At the right time, the player can descend into the borehole and grab a luminous mineral so they can see within the [[Glacial Caverns]].
