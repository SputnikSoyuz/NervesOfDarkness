Same for Revealed Title

| Rumour Reveal Location                         | Rumour Description                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enclosed Caverns Entrance                      | The caverns are too hot to enter!                                                             |
| Plasma Blast Furnace Turn Off                  | I turned off the Plasma Blast Furnace and the power went out. It also feels a lot colder now. |
| **Visiting Caverns After Turning Off Furnace** | **After turning off the Plasma Blast Furnace, I could safely enter the Torrid Caverns.**      |
The Torrid Caverns is the path to the Warped Observatory. It can only be entered if the player disables the Plasma Blast Furnace, otherwise the player will burn to death. However, the player can only reach the Warped Observatory if they cross the path during the big split.
