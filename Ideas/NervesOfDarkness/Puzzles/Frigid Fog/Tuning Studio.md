The Tuning Studio is the central puzzle of Frigid Fog, located inside an impassable hurricane on the North Pole of the planet. To reach there, you need to gain access to the Jackhammer Item on Purple Partition's far side and find the path to it at the Maroon Meadows Botany Lab. The Tuning Studio shows you how to find your way through the inner layer to the [[Conductive Energy Plant]].
