Nerves of Darkness would need a team for the mod to realise its full potential.

**Voice Actors**
Voice Actors will be needed for various cryptic language puzzles, and some of the lore recordings late in the game. Things they will need to do:
	- Language Puzzle: Help make a custom phonetic language, mostly just record the lines.
	- Lore Recordings: Read aloud pieces of the lore, which will be brought into audible form.

**Writers**
Writers will be needed to write dialogue, Ship Logs, help form the mod's story, and make sure my writing is expressed clearly and accurately. Nerves of Darkness doesn't have too much dialogue outside of Ship Logs and the Mod Intro, so writers will not be expected of much.
	- Editing My Writing: Using whatever words I hurl out and make sure it makes actual sense and fits nicely in with base game and the mod's canon. I'm not the best writer when it comes to non-technical things such as story and characters, and sometimes I can make no sense at all.
	- Character Development: Write dialogue for specific characters that already exist (such as Chert), or potentially new ones as well. Make sure their writing make sense for the character to say.
	- Ship Log Writing: Help write the Ship Logs in ways the player can thoroughly understand and connect them in ways that make sense.

**Modelers**
Modelers will model all the 3D objects in the game, and will likely have quite a bit of things to get done. Planets, props, structures, and new species will need to be 3D modelled. 
	- Custom Kit: Props and structures will likely be constructed by a kit similar to what the Inhabitants and Nomai kits have. The new species needs its own kit.
	- Model for Planets: Purple Partition, Frigid Fog and Manic Mesa need to be modelled entirely because they have complex cave systems and other unique things. Purple Partition, it needs to split itself in half somewhat throughout the loop (I will be doing that via animation).
	- Custom Buildings: Several structures will likely need more than just a kit to be made, such as space bases and other interesting structures.
	- Custom Props: Several props and items will likely also need modelled.
	- Armature of New Species: An armature of the new species will need to be made. Leg animations are not necessary, just the arms, body, and head.

**2D Artist**
2D Artists will draw stuff for the mod. As of right now, the only thing I can think of that needs drawn are the Ship Log icons and the New Universe ending, but some other things might come along.

**Programming Assistant**
Although not required, programming assistance is welcome with open arms. They will most likely help with programming and improving code obfuscation and performance.

**Beta Testers**
Beta Testers will test puzzles and other various things throughout the mod's development.


**How Many Do We Need?**
Voice Actors: 3-5 (for variance)
Writers: 1-3
Modelers: 2-4
Programming Assistant: 0-2
Beta Testers: 3-10
